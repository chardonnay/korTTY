    korTTY Guide¶

    The complete reference for korTTY — a modern, cross-platform JavaFX SSH client with tabbed terminals, SFTP tooling, a background job scheduler, snippet management, terminal-effect plugins, integrated local GGUF inference and OpenAI-compatible AI assistance. Every feature, setting and tool, explained in detail.
